Our lab researches systems software that supports social infrastructure　—　the unsung heroes such as operating systems and virtual machines.
We aim to implement systems software that maximizes performance and makes computers more convenient and secure through abstraction of advanced functions and complex controls.

We also strive to share our research outcomes not only within the academic field but also with open-source software communities.
